# DT60 Dump Truck Specification

Document ID: SPEC-DT60
Machine type: mining dump truck

## Payload and weight

- Rated payload: 60 t
- Curb weight: 42 t
- Body volume (heaped): 36 m3
- Maximum speed: 50 km/h

## Power and brake

- Engine rated power: 448 kW
- Drive: 6x4
- Brake: dual-circuit air brake plus exhaust brake
